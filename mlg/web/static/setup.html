Set up Muse Life Graph

Muse Life Graph runs on this computer and reads your photos where they are. It needs two things of your own: a key for Meta's models, and a free graph database. You pay Meta directly for what Muse reads (about $0.006 a photo; the app shows the estimate before it spends anything).

1 · Your Meta Model API key

  1. Open dev.meta.ai and sign in.
  2. Create an API key and copy it.

2 · Your graph database (free)

  1. Open console.neo4j.io and create a free AuraDB Free instance.
  2. Download its credentials file when it offers it: it has the address and the password.

3 · Muse Spark Open on Baseten (optional)

With a Baseten key, search, memory titles and the picture checker run on Meta's open-weights model. Without one, everything uses your Meta key.

Your keys are saved only on this computer, in your app folder, and sent only to the services they belong to. Your photos never leave this computer except as the model calls themselves.
