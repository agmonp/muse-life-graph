Muse Life Graph for Windows
===========================

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (for example Documents\MuseLifeGraph). Keep the files together.
2. Open MuseLifeGraph.exe. A small window says where it runs; your browser opens the app.
   Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because this free app is not code-signed yet:
   choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. The first screen asks for two things of your own:
   - a Meta Model API key (https://dev.meta.ai) - you pay Meta directly, about $0.006 a photo;
     the app shows the estimate before it spends anything;
   - a free graph database: Neo4j AuraDB Free (https://console.neo4j.io).
   Optional: a Baseten key, to run search and titles on Meta's open-weights Muse Spark Open.
4. Choose "Add photos": a folder on this computer, or the .zip files of your Facebook / Instagram download.

Your keys are saved only in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MuseLifeGraph\settings.env, and the app's caches and films
next to it. Your photos are read where they are and never leave this computer except as the model calls
themselves. To stop the app, close its window. To remove it, delete this folder and %LOCALAPPDATA%\MuseLifeGraph.

Music and narration (Meta MusicGen and SeamlessM4T) need an NVIDIA GPU and are not part of this download;
without them films get on-screen captions. See the README to add them.

Source, license (Apache-2.0) and help: https://github.com/agmonp/muse-life-graph
The ffmpeg folder holds FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org), GPL build by BtbN (https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds);
its license is in ffmpeg\LICENSE.txt and its source is available from those pages.
